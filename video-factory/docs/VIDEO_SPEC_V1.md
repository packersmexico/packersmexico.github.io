# VideoSpec V1

Required fields:

- `version`: `1.0`
- `pmxId`
- `family`: `ANALYSIS | BREAKING | MATCHUP`
- `mode`: `HERO | TEASER | HOOK | STORY`
- `aspect`: `9:16 | 4:5 | 1:1`
- `headline`
- `durationSeconds`

Optional:

- `kicker`
- `subheadline`
- `body`
- `cta`
- `sourceLabel`
- `destinationLabel`
- `audioUrl`
- `assetUrl`
- `assetCredit`
- `captions[]`

## Locks
The factory must receive already-approved content. It may not:
- change facts;
- strengthen certainty;
- substitute an asset;
- alter PMX identity;
- remove attribution/cautions for fit;
- publish.
